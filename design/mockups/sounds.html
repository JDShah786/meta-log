How they're built

  • If it moves, it makes a sound: taps click, screens ratchet, sheets slide like paper.
  • Clicks, not thuds: tiny 5–15 ms snaps, each panned a touch left or right, so they sit close in your ears.
  • Two-part clicks: select is "ka-chick", back is "chick-ka", LOG is "ka-chick-chick" into a soft chime.
  • Pitch means something: select and confirm rise, back and remove fall, errors are two dull taps.
  • Beads are tiny glass ticks climbing a musical scale, so a big log runs up to the rank.
  • Everything is generated in code. No recordings, nothing borrowed from a game.
